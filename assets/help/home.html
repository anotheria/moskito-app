MoSKito Help

Welcome to the MoSKito Monitoring App! If this is your first time here, we're glad to have you.

This is the main screen of the application. Its primary goal is to give you easy access to the statuses of all components that are part of your system.

A typical MoSKito-Control instance organizes components into views. Each view has its own status, which reflects the worst status among all components in that view.

The screen displays views and their components. The status is indicated by a small colored bubble in front of each component or view.

There are the following status indicators, typically interpreted as:

  • Green – Everything is OK.
  • Yellow – Slightly abnormal parameters, e.g., a spike in CPU usage.
  • Orange – Abnormal parameters, e.g., a prolonged spike in CPU usage or a high number of threads.
  • Red – Potential disturbance in the system; requires attention.
  • Purple – Component is non-functioning or unreachable; immediate attention is required.

You can tap on any component to open an inspection window, which consists of four tabs:

  • Thresholds – All thresholds defined in the component, including their current status and value (tap on threshold names for details).
  • Charts – A list of available charts for the component (tap to view).
  • Information – Connector and component details, such as uptime, JVM version, component location, and the type of connector used.
  • Alert History – Alerts related to the component, showing previous and current statuses, timestamps, and related messages (tap for details).

Finally, in the lower-right corner of the screen, you'll find the system selector button, which allows you to switch between multiple systems.
